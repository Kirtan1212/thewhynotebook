The Why Notebook

Hand-drawn explainers about the science hiding in everyday things: why a cricket ball swings, how golf balls got their dimples, how a melted chocolate bar invented the microwave.

New videos every week, in three series: Sport science, Engineering behind… and Everyday physics.

Watch on YouTube: @thewhynotebookyt

How the videos are made

Every script is researched from published sources, which are listed in each video's description, and reviewed by a person before it's produced. The narration uses an AI voice, and the drawings are generated in code in a notebook style.
